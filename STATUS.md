# Status

[Version 1.0.0](https://github.com/DannyExperiments/crossing-free-hypergraph-lines/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · 27 November 2026 · [Version DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24569558)

The recognition problem is ∃ℝ-complete even for linear hypergraphs with pairwise intersecting hyperedges. The manuscript gives the corollary of Kim, de Mesmay and Miltzow’s theorem using projective line duality and records the relevant source distinctions.

The proof has undergone internal review. External peer review and formalization are pending. The [literature audit](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md) records the sources and limits of the check. The README links the repository, replay and PDF build workflows.
