# Claim scope and limitations

The input is a finite hypergraph with distinct complete affine lines for its hyperedges and distinct plane points for its vertices. The reduction proves ∃ℝ-completeness even when every pair of distinct hyperedges shares exactly one vertex. In this output family, reconstructing the underlying simple rank-three matroid uses their shared vertices to recover all dependent triples.

The fixed rank is the input matroid’s rank. The construction allows growing hyperedge size and vertex degree. Dobler et al.’s polynomial cases for hypergraph rank at most two or maximum vertex degree at most two concern different restrictions. The claim concerns real coordinates and complete lines. Segment models, drawing-order constraints and rational realizability are outside this note’s scope. [Source special cases](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.13985v4#S5.SS1)

The note states a corollary of a published theorem. The finite replay tests the proof’s construction on exact samples. External peer review and formalization are pending.
